Zoom out

  • Opens smoother. The fly-in moves each window card as one piece, so nothing is redrawn while it flies. Card shadows are drawn by macOS itself.
  • Window pictures update one card at a time instead of redrawing all of Zoom out.
  • Its glass shelf and title pills are drawn from a picture Mondo fits to macOS's own glass. Mondo measures the glass once, off screen, for your wallpaper or theme. Where it can't match closely, Zoom out keeps the real glass.
  • Esc and ⇪Esc always close it. It also closes itself after a minute with no input.

Never stuck

  • Mondo can't keep your keyboard. If a panel that holds the keys isn't actually showing, the keys come back within a third of a second. If Mondo ever stalls, your keys go straight through.
  • If Mondo stalls for over 2 seconds, it writes what it was doing to ~/Library/Logs/Mondo/hangs.log.

Moving windows between desktops

  • Fixed: a window moved many times crept right until the move failed at the screen's edge. A moved window now lands where it was.

Settings

  • Shortcuts is the All shortcuts sheet (⇪/), editable. Click any row or tile to set a key. A key that's already used offers Swap. Search dims what doesn't match.
  • Settings opens wide enough for two columns.
  • Small live pictures explain jump hints, Zoom out, tab groups, the palette and This Mac.
  • Edges' on/off sits in the page title. Tab groups and Clipboard history have theirs in their section headers.
  • Quicklinks line up in columns, with readable icons, edit and remove on hover, and drag to reorder.
  • This Mac is now just the Kit and "Changes Mondo made" (with Put back).
  • App rules include your former pins. A rule's desktop is the app's macOS "Assign to Desktop".

Also

  • The menu bar icon no longer changes per desktop. It has two looks: keep awake off, and on.
  • The ⇪/ sheet fits short screens.
